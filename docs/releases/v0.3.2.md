Eight new themes, a closed notch you can click into and resize in place, and a round of security and reliability fixes.

- Themes: Catppuccin Mocha and Latte, Nord, Rosé Pine and Rosé Pine Dawn, Gruvbox Dark, Solarized Light and Everforest join the seven there were, three more of them light. Settings → Themes.
- The closed notch opens where you point: a click on the readings opens Hardware, on the AI rings AI Usage, on the song the music, on the timer or the clock the Timer, on the next event the Calendar. With Open on hover, the part under the pointer decides the same way.
- Resize the closed notch, capsule or line on the notch itself: Settings → Display → Adjust on the notch, then drag its ends and its far side, and Done.
- What's new: after an update, or when a new version is out, a line in the closed notch says so and opens the changes, with a short note on supporting Crest.
- The converter opens on the units and currencies you used last.
- Weather keeps the last forecast for when Open-Meteo doesn't answer, and says how old it is.
- Security: Crest's windows stay on their own pages, a dropped link no longer opens in the notch, the timer and mirror windows can only call their own commands, and Explorer is started by its full path.
- Fixes: the vault no longer looks damaged while another program holds its file, and turning on Windows Hello twice in a row can't lose it; vault, plugin storage and portfolio history are flushed to disk before they are replaced; a stopped Ask question no longer breaks the next one; portfolio amounts like "1,000" read as a thousand; Shelf text is hidden while presenting; a timer that ran out while Crest was closed doesn't ring at login; Discord no longer reopens its consent dialog every few seconds.
