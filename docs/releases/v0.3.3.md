Coloured icons, a notch that answers your coding agent, and a dozen new tools and options picked from what other notch apps do well.

- Coloured icons: each tool in the tool bar, each Settings page, the weather, the Overview tiles, the Hardware rows and the closed-row readings get a colour of their own. The colours are worked out for the theme you use, so they keep 3:1 contrast on light and custom themes too. Settings → Themes → Coloured icons; the old switch in Hardware moved here and keeps its value.
- Permission requests: `crest-notify ask` shows a question with Allow, Always allow and Deny in the notch and waits for your answer. docs/notices.md has a ready-made hook for a coding agent's permission requests.
- Calendar: events with a Google Meet, Zoom, Teams or Webex link get a Join button, in the agenda and in the notice before the event.
- Overview: a Keep awake tile (until you turn it off, or for 30 minutes, 1 or 2 hours, with a cup in the closed notch while it is on), and a button on the volume tile that picks the audio output.
- Hardware: the Windows power plan in the Power row when the PC has more than one, each process's CPU share in percent, and temperatures and power from HWiNFO or LibreHardwareMonitor while one of them runs.
- Caps Lock and Num Lock show in the notch when you press them, like the volume.
- Timer and clock: Flip, Nixie, Ring, LCD and Analog join the seven looks there were, and Settings → Timer picks a colour for the focus timer (orange stays the default, breaks stay green) and one for the Overview clock.
- Timer: focus sounds (white, pink and brown noise, rain, waves) that play while a focus timer runs and fade out when it ends. Settings → Timer.
- Settings search: type in the field above the pages, or press Ctrl+F while Settings is open.
- Music: Spectrum and Wave equalizers and a Wavy progress bar (Settings → Display → Music), and a soft tint of the album art's colour on the open panel (Settings → Themes).
- Camera: a new tool, off by default, to check yourself before a call. The picture stays on your screen and is never recorded.
- Find on screen: Ctrl+Alt+F reads every screen with Windows' own text recognition and lets you search and copy what is on them. Nothing is saved or sent.
- Downloads: a browser download into your Downloads folder shows its size and speed in the closed notch, and a notice offers Show in folder and Add to Shelf when it is done.
- Notices: a Windows notice opens its app on a click, and deleting it in Crest removes it from the Windows notification centre too.
- Fixes: menus keep each choice on one line, so long window titles in Capture no longer overlap.
- Store version: Settings → Support and the What's new page have a button to rate Crest in the Microsoft Store.
