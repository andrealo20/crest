A month in the Calendar, the clock in any timer look, Bluetooth notices for every device and a first Mac preview.

- Calendar: a month next to the agenda, with a dot for each calendar on the days that have events. Pick a day to see the agenda from there; the arrows go one month back and two ahead, and the month name brings you back to today.
- Clock look: the Overview clock can wear any of the timer looks, from Zen to HUD. Settings → Clock look. The idea came from RobbieIsTheName on Reddit.
- Bluetooth: the connect and disconnect notice now covers every Bluetooth device (headphones, mice, keyboards, controllers, speakers, phones), with its battery when the device reports one. Headphones never showed on PCs with an audio offload driver, AMD's among them; that is fixed. The setting is now called Bluetooth devices.
- Smoother: opening the notch and switching tools no longer blur the whole panel, which cost a long frame on every open on high-DPI screens.
- Mac preview: disk images for Apple silicon and Intel Macs. This is a first build, not notarized and barely tried on real hardware, so expect rough edges and please open an issue for what breaks. Shelf, Hardware, Discord, Vault, Terminal, Launcher, Notices and Capture are Windows-only for now and don't show on a Mac.
