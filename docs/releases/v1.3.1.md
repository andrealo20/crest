Crest's taskbar now does most of what Windows' own does, and the notch gets new options. Every item is described in [features.md](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/blob/main/docs/features.md).

**Taskbar** (Windows, not in the Microsoft Store version)

- Jump lists, Windows' app menu, and the badges and progress apps report.
- Pin any open app or a dropped .exe, .lnk or .url ([#74](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/74)), and drag pins into a new order.
- Win+1 to Win+9, Win+T and Win+B ([#71](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/71)), the bar on the top edge ([#73](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/73)), and buttons combined or with labels.
- Windows' Search, Task view, Input language, Privacy indicators and Recycle Bin buttons, and a volume mixer.
- A Disk space widget whose Scan now finds the largest folders.
- The clock opens Crest's notices and calendar.
- A red bin removes a dragged item, and the Start button has six icons to choose from.

**Notch**

- The tool bar can sit at the top and lose its background, and each part of Overview has its own switch ([#76](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/76)).
- A frames per second slider for the live glass, and a live Liquid Glass + tool bar when the taskbar uses it.
- Text size in Note and Calc ([#68](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/68)), Core Temp for the CPU temperature ([#67](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/67)), and apps from a file in the Launcher ([#70](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/70), [#75](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/75)).

**Fixes**

- The hidden icons panel ([#65](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/65)), Windows' pins with no Start entry ([#74](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/74)), the wheel on the Shelf ([#66](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/66)), Now Playing getting too short ([#64](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/64)) and far fewer disk writes from AI Usage.
