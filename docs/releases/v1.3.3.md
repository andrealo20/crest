Crest reads the text and the QR code in a copied image, converts one amount into several currencies at once and lets the arrow keys move between tools. The taskbar asks before emptying the Recycle Bin in a panel of its own and puts Search beside Start, and many fixes reach both. Every item is described in [features.md](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/blob/main/docs/features.md).

**Notch**

- Clipboard: the preview of a copied image reads its text (**Copy text**) and its QR code: a link opens in the browser, any other content is copied. It all happens on this PC. On a Mac only the QR code.
- Converter: one amount into up to eight currencies at once, and a click on a row brings that currency to the top ([#87](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/87)).
- Left and Right move between tools while the notch is open, and Alt+Left/Right inside a text field (Cmd+Option on a Mac) ([#99](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/99)).
- **Opens on** chooses what the notch opens on: the live activity, the tool open last or one tool every time ([#87](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/87)).
- The Launcher gets an icon size and a switch for the names ([#87](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/87)), and brings an app that is already open forward instead of starting a second copy ([#64](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/64)).
- The line's **Colour** can be the closed notch's, white or the accent, and Clocks finds capitals and big cities that keep another city's time, Brasília or Washington, with or without accents ([#87](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/87)).
- Lyrics are found when Spotify's **Crossfade songs** reports each song a few seconds short ([#98](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/98)).

**Taskbar** (Windows, not in the Microsoft Store version)

- Emptying the Recycle Bin asks once, in a panel of Crest's, from its button and its menu.
- Search gets a magnifier with a sparkle and goes beside Start when added, and the Widgets page lists Windows' buttons, each with Add or Remove.
- **Input language** also shows for Chinese, Japanese and Korean input methods on a single keyboard layout ([#99](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/99)).
- Windows' own volume, network and battery icons stay out of the tray, where a click on them did nothing; Stato shows the same ([#99](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/99)).
- The bar steps aside while Windows' search panel covers it, and the settings that keep Crest out of captures say that NVIDIA's Instant Replay switches off with them ([#88](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/88)).

**Fixes**

- A window that did not come forward on a click now does, the way Alt+Tab brings it, and its button says so if Windows still refuses ([#94](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/94)).
- A tray icon's menu stays above the bar ([#91](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/91)), white tray icons stay visible on a light theme ([#93](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/93)), and Crest keeps the tray when another program such as lumabar has one too.
- Weather's **Automatic** location can be chosen again after **Manual**, and Settings says when the place cannot be found ([#99](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/99)).
- The bars follow Windows' Text size, the notices' count sits between the clock and its weather, **Clear all** is named in the notices' header, and windows under the notch follow the maximized rule ([#90](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/pull/90)).
- A preview's click after a drag, the jump list, the drives after an eject, the Recycle Bin's count, the quick panel's page and files dropped on an app's button all behave again.
- Smoother: panels fade in without a blur, a hover redraws only the buttons it moves between, the notch stays still during a Discord call, and an endless progress rests while the bar is hidden.
