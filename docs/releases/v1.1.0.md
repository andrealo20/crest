A shortcut for every tool, a colour picker of Crest's own, backups, paste straight from the Clipboard history, web search in Overview and local AI models in Ask, plus fixes for the issues you reported after 1.0.

**New**

- Tool shortcuts: **Open a tool directly**, under the shortcuts in Settings → Window, adds a shortcut for any tool in the dock. It opens the notch on that tool from anywhere, switches to it while the notch shows another, and closes the notch when that tool already shows ([#19](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/19)).
- Paste directly (Settings → Tools → Clipboard, off by default): a click on an entry in the Clipboard pastes it into the app you were in, like Win + V. On a Mac Crest asks for the Accessibility permission the first time ([#19](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/19)).
- Colour: **Pick from screen** now uses Crest's own eyedropper on Windows. A loupe over a still picture of all your monitors shows the pixels around the pointer and the value of the middle one, in the real pixels of every monitor whatever its scaling. The arrow keys move by one pixel and Enter picks. On a Mac the system's colour sampler does the same, so the button is there too ([#24](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/24)).
- Backup (Settings → General): save your settings, notes, to-dos and every tool's data to one file in Documents › Crest › Backups, and restore it on this PC or another one. Before a restore Crest saves the data it replaces as a backup of its own. Keys, passwords and the vault stay out of the file.
- Web search in Overview (Settings → Tools, off by default) with Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo or Brave Search ([#27](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/27)).
- Ask works with a model on your own PC through LM Studio or Ollama, with no key and no account: choose the custom endpoint and enter its local address ([#27](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/27)).

**New in the tools you have**

- The panel scale goes from 80 to 200 percent in eight steps, in Settings → Window ([#14](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/14)).
- Now Playing can show the album cover, blurred and darkened, behind the whole panel. The lyrics, the mixer and the player list now add their room to the size you chose, so the panel stays resizable while they are open.
- Converter and Calc: about 160 currencies from the European Central Bank and other central banks, the Vietnamese dong among them. Each rate keeps the day its bank published it ([#25](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/25)).

**Fixes**

- A file or text dragged out of the Shelf now drops into the app below the panel instead of getting lost in Crest ([#22](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/22)).
- With Hide Windows banners on, the empty banner window Windows still shows for a few seconds no longer catches the pointer or keeps an auto-hidden taskbar from coming up ([#23](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/23)).
- A new song starts at zero when the player keeps the last song's timeline, so its lyrics no longer jump to the end ([#26](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/26)).
- The Unlock FPS row no longer jumps while the FPS counter retries ([#17](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/17)).

Try every tool in your browser before installing: [andrealo20.github.io/crest](https://andrealo20.github.io/crest/).
