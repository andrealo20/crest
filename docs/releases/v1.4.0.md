The bar's search box finds apps, settings, files and answers, and with Ask it does what a sentence says. Pomelo comes into Crest: the Files tool converts and edits images, PDFs, audio and video, and Pomelo's wheel works on files dragged in File Explorer. Also a Translate tool, stocks in the crypto widget, a notch that can stay behind other windows and what you asked for after 1.3.3. Every item is described in [features.md](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/blob/main/docs/features.md).

**Search or open** (taskbar, Windows, not in the Microsoft Store version)

- The bar's search box finds apps, also by their initials ("vsc" for Visual Studio Code), Crest's tools, settings by name with a value or a step ("height 48", "volume 30"), widgets to put on the bar or take off it, and files and folders from Windows' index, with a preview of the selected photo or video.
- Quick answers: sums, currency and unit conversions and the time in a city ("time in tokyo"). Anything else goes to the web, in your default browser.
- With a provider set up in **Ask**, a sentence in any language becomes an action ("set the volume to 30"). Without one, the empty panel offers **Turn on AI**.
- A shortcut of your choice opens the search from any app. The panel's glow along its edge has a switch.
- Apps stay on the bar until it is really full: the centre moves aside before any app goes behind "…".

**Pomelo** (Windows)

- **Files**, a new tool (off until you turn it on in Settings → Tools): drop images, PDFs, audio or video on it, or take them from the Shelf, and convert or edit them with [Pomelo](https://pomeloapp.net)'s conversions and tools. The originals are never changed; the results land beside them or on the Shelf.
- **Pomelo wheel**, in the new Pomelo scope of the settings window (off by default): drag files in File Explorer and hold Shift, Ctrl or Alt, and a wheel with Pomelo's conversions appears.
- Both run on Pomelo's engine, a separate closed-source program that is not part of Crest and not under its GPL-3.0. The GitHub versions download it only when you press **Download the program** and check it against a fingerprint written in Crest's code; the Microsoft Store version carries it inside its package.
- 20 files are free, then 2 a day. A key costs US$4.99 once, a launch offer until 31 December 2026 (Pomelo alone costs US$9.99), and works in every version of Crest that has the tool. **Buy** opens Stripe's checkout in your browser, and **Open in Crest** on the thanks page fills in the key. Files are converted on your PC and never sent anywhere.

**Notch**

- **Translate**, a new tool: text translated on the PC with the same offline models as the lyrics, in ten languages, or by the Ask provider when you allow it.
- The tool bar shows the selected tool's name beside its icon, or over it when there's no room, and scrolls when it's full.
- **Behind other windows** (Settings → Window) keeps the notch on the desktop, under every window. The shortcut still brings it to the front.
- The **floating timer** follows the window rules like the notch ([#95](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/95)), thanks to Anton Matis.
- **Hibernate** joins Overview's power buttons when hibernation is on in Windows ([#103](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/103)).
- **Material** gets Pure black for dark themes and Material's own shapes, and its Hardware tool lists every drive.

**Taskbar** (Windows, not in the Microsoft Store version)

- Battery, Wi-Fi and volume drawn as on an iPhone and a Mac: the battery green while charging, yellow in Energy saver and red when low, with its percentage beside it (a switch in Taskbar → General).
- The crypto widget also shows a stock, fund or index by its Yahoo Finance symbol (AAPL, VWCE.DE, ^GSPC), or your Portfolio's total, over today, a week, a month or a year.
- Floating, Capsule and Tiles get **Keep room for the bar**: maximized windows end above the bar and its gap, as with Docked ([#108](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/108)). With it on and Auto-hide off, Start opens above the bar over a full-screen game too.
- **Only on the screen you're working on**, under Auto-hide: only the bar of the screen where the app in front is slides away, and the other bars stay over their windows ([#97](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/97)).

**Fixes**

- With **Windows keys on the bar** on, Crest no longer restarts Explorer as it starts. On some Windows builds that restart crashed the taskbar, at every start ([#104](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/104)).
- The Recycle Bin widget stays up to date after Explorer restarts ([#107](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/107)).

**Count this install**

Once a day Crest sends its version, Windows or macOS, and where it came from (Store, GitHub or portable) to crestisland.app. There is no ID and nothing else; the country is read from the IP for the count, and the IP is not stored. The totals are public at [crestisland.app/stats](https://crestisland.app/stats/). **Count this install** in Settings → General → Updates turns it off.

Try every tool in your browser before installing: [crestisland.app](https://crestisland.app/).
