The biggest update so far: six new tools, break reminders, files to your phone by QR code, offline lyric translation, a pace line for AI limits, and fixes for more than 150 bugs found in a full review of the app on Windows x64, ARM64 and the Mac.

Every new tool is off until you turn it on in Settings → Tools.

**New tools**

- Calc: a notebook calculator, one calculation per line with the result on the right. It reads units (`5 km in miles`, `72 F in C`), percentages (`20% of 80`), variables, dates and currencies, and a click on a result copies it.
- Clocks: world clocks with a slider that moves the time up to a day either way, so you can see what 3 pm here is for everyone, plus sunrise, sunset, golden hour and the moon for your weather place.
- Colour: pick a colour from anywhere on the screen or paste one, copy it as HEX, RGB, HSL or OKLCH, check its contrast against another and see its harmonies.
- Habits: up to 20 habits, every day or on the weekdays you pick, with five weeks of dots and a streak that forgives a missed day now and then.
- Prompter: a teleprompter that scrolls your script at the speed you set, with a countdown, a mirror mode for glass and keys to move around while you read.
- Agents: the Claude Code sessions running on this PC, with their state, what each one is waiting for, and a button that copies the command to resume one.

**New in the tools you have**

- Break reminders (Settings → Timer → Breaks): an eye break every 20 minutes and a longer break every hour, counted from the time you actually spend at the computer. They wait during presentations, full-screen apps, recordings and Do Not Disturb.
- Shelf: Send to phone shows a QR code. Scan it and the phone's browser downloads the files straight from the PC, over your local network only.
- Lyrics: Translate offline translates songs on your PC with the models Firefox uses for page translation, into the language you pick. The closed notch can show the translated or the romanised line instead of the original ([#10](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/10)).
- AI usage: a pace line under each limit says where it will stand at the reset, or when you would hit it at this rate. An optional notice tells you when a limit has reset. Antigravity is read from its app, and the Claude card works with a claude.ai session key for those who use the desktop app without Claude Code ([#9](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/9)).
- Notices: a notice with a one-time code gets a Copy code button. Hide Windows banners shows each notification once, in the notch ([#6](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/6)).
- Clipboard: decode chips name a JWT, a Unix time, Base64, a UUID, JSON, a colour or a link with tracking, and show what it decodes to. Everything stays on the PC.
- To-dos: type "call Marco tomorrow 9am" or "venerdì alle 17" and the task gets that due time. English and Italian for now.
- Hardware: a sheet lists the ports your PC is listening on, with the program behind each one, and can end a process of yours.
- Hide Crest from screen sharing and recordings (Settings → Display → Presentation mode), a microphone mute among the quick controls with its own shortcut, and a closed notch that can show only the network speed ([#11](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/11)).

**Fixes**

- Crest pauses its readings while the screen is off or the PC sleeps, and no longer talks to a laptop's own panel over DDC/CI. Both could take part in a crash on wake from standby on some laptops.
- The panel stays open when the dock switches to a smaller tool and the pointer is left outside it ([#12](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/12)).
- Bluetooth headphones no longer drop out while music plays ([#7](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/7)), and the layout problems on some screens are fixed ([#4](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/4), [#5](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/5)).
- A full review of the app fixed more than 150 smaller bugs: keyboard use and screen readers, Arabic and other long translations, the weather card, the calendar, the vault, the portfolio, captures, the Mac version and the installer. Crest also keeps working when its saved data is damaged.

Try every tool in your browser before installing: [andrealo20.github.io/crest](https://andrealo20.github.io/crest/).
