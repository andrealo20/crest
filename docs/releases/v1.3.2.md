Crest's taskbar gets folders for your pinned apps, many new widgets and a size slider for them, and pinned tools now open right above the bar. The notch gets Screen Time and wallpapers. Every item is described in [features.md](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/blob/main/docs/features.md).

**Taskbar** (Windows, not in the Microsoft Store version)

- Folders: hold a pinned app over another for a moment and both go into a folder, which opens in a panel over the bar. Open apps from a folder still show on the bar.
- New widgets and faces: top apps by CPU or memory, battery rings and cells, a network graph, Now Playing with cover and controls, the microphone, a stopwatch, the next to-do, the latest note, day, month and year progress, the date, world time, flip and segment clocks, time and weather, weather details, the next days, sunrise and sunset, currencies, crypto, Screen Time, the day timeline, unread notifications, device batteries, temperatures with a 10-minute line, Keep awake and a wallpaper switcher.
- Windows buttons for light or dark theme, the on-screen keyboard, the notification center, Do not disturb, recent files, a search box, desktop icons, Eject and the mobile hotspot.
- A Widget size slider, and a button that opens the widgets the bar has no room for. The clock always stays on the bar.
- Pinned tools such as Portfolio, Calc, Clipboard, Ask, the Launcher and the Recorder open in a panel over the bar instead of the notch.
- On the Time and weather clock, a click on the weather opens Weather.
- The Crest button can be removed ([#83](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/83)), and windows on a monitor without a bar show on the main one ([#84](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/84)).

**Notch**

- Screen Time, off until you turn it on: the time you spend in each app, kept only on this PC for 30 days. The Day timeline shows your focus rounds, events, sunrise and sunset.
- Wallpapers: pick a picture from Crest's collection or from a folder of your own, for each screen, and change it on a timer. The collection's pictures are downloaded the first time you use them.
- The battery of your Bluetooth devices in the closed notch.
- The Timer goes up to 24 hours, keeps your last three durations as chips and has a stopwatch with laps.
- Hardware shows your local IP (a click opens ipconfig) and, only when you ask, your public IP. A click on the battery health opens Windows' battery report ([#85](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/85)), and the battery health now shows in Material too.
- The equalizer uses much less CPU ([#81](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/81)), and the tool bar keeps its width so its icons stay put when you switch tools ([#82](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/82)).

**Fixes**

- No empty Crest card in Alt+Tab ([#78](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/78)), the Animal row is turned off with the Line shape and says why ([#80](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/80)), the bar no longer re-reserves its space in a loop, a likely cause of twitching desktop icons ([#79](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/79)), and the Downloads preview in Settings shows both of its states ([#72](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/72)).
