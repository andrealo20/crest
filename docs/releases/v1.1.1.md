**New**

- Crest can play a sound of its own when a notice pops up in the notch, at the moment the card appears: one of ten short sounds, from a ding to a water drop, a popping balloon and a knock on wood, or a file of your own (MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A or FLAC). While it is on, Windows' own sound stays off for the apps Crest shows, and it comes back when you turn the sound off or quit Crest. Settings → Notices → Sound ([#31](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/31)).
- Notices from desktop programs such as Chrome and Discord show the app's own icon instead of its first letter, the icons on the Notices page are larger, and the cards in the notch no longer have a coloured line along the bottom ([#31](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/31)).
- The Vault's password form has a Copy button. It copies the password in the field, saved or just generated, out of every clipboard history, and the clipboard is emptied after 30 seconds.

**Changed**

- Focus mode is gone. Three quick clicks on the notch turned it half transparent and let clicks through, and once on by mistake the notch looked broken. Now the notch simply opens.

**Fixes**

- A notice whose app withdraws it, such as a WhatsApp message you read in WhatsApp, or that you clear in Windows' notification centre, now leaves Crest too ([#30](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/30)).
- Switching to a smaller tool no longer closes the notch when the pointer ends up outside the new panel, whether you switch with the tool bar, the mouse wheel or a link inside a tool such as the Overview tiles ([#29](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/29)).

Try every tool in your browser before installing: [crestisland.app/try](https://crestisland.app/try/).
