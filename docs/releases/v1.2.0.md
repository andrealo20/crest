Liquid Glass: the open panel, the closed notch and the capsule can now be glass that bends and blurs the screen behind them. Also themes that change at sunrise and sunset, the date beside the clock, and what you asked for after 1.1.

**New**

- Liquid Glass (Settings → Themes → Glass, off by default, Windows): the open panel becomes glass. The screen behind it shows through, bent along the sides, blurred in the middle, with a light rim, and the theme still decides the text, which keeps its contrast over any backdrop. It works from the notch or the line on the top edge and on the capsule. Four sliders set Transparency, Refraction, Frost and Rim light. Glass turns itself off with a contrast theme, with transparency effects off or energy saver on, in presentation mode and over a full-screen app.
- Glass on the closed notch and the capsule (**Also the closed notch and capsule**, under Glass, off by default; Windows 11 24H2 or later on x64, not in the Microsoft Store version for now): they show a live copy of the screen just around them, renewed up to ten times a second. While the switch is on, the notch is left out of every screenshot, recording and screen share, and Crest uses more battery; the setting says so.
- The tool bar's highlight follows the pointer: it slides to the tool you point at, grows under it, squashes on a click and bounces back, and it follows the keyboard too. Over the glass it is a drop of glass on a capsule of clear glass; without it, and on the Mac, a brighter pill that moves the same way.
- Day and night themes can change at sunrise and sunset (Settings → Themes → Day and night → **At sunrise and sunset**), at the place set for the Weather. Crest works the two times out on your computer ([#41](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/41)).
- The clock in the closed notch can show the date (Settings → Display, under Clock) ([#41](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/41)).

**New in the tools you have**

- A click on a Windows notice in the closed notch opens its app right away ([#34](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/34)).
- Overview has play/pause and next beside the song ([#35](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/35)).

**Changed**

- Synced lyrics in the closed notch stay through pauses shorter than 10 seconds, so the notch no longer shrinks and grows between lines ([#37](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/37)).
- With the capsule as the closed look, notices open as the capsule, also when a window rule turns it into the line, and their cards fit closer.
- Crest looks for updates every 4 hours instead of once a day.

**Fixes**

- A favourite's tooltip in Edge or Chrome no longer stays on screen while Crest is open ([#40](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/40)).
- The red privacy dot now lights while Snipping Tool records the screen ([#36](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/pull/36)).
