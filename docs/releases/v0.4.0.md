A Recorder for sound, a brightness slider in Overview, AI usage that says when its numbers are old, and notices that open what they show.

- Recorder: a new tool that records sound only, from the microphone, the system audio or both mixed, so a call keeps your voice and everyone else's. Files are M4A in Music\Crest\Recordings, listed in the tool with their length, and pause, stop and the timer in the closed notch work as for screen recordings. It is off by default: turn it on in Settings, Tools. Crest tells no one that you are recording; the first time you open it, a note reminds you that in many countries a conversation may only be recorded with everyone's consent.
- Brightness: Overview has a slider for the screen the notch is on, over DDC/CI on external monitors and through Windows on a laptop's own screen. On a Mac it sets the built-in display. The row shows only where Crest can set the brightness.
- AI usage: when a reading is out of date, the tool says why and dims the old limits instead of showing them as current. When the sign-in of the command line tool has expired, Renew in the terminal starts it in Crest's terminal, which refreshes the sign-in.
- Notices: a click on a notice opens what it is about, not the part of the closed notch it covers. The What's new line opens What's new instead of Hardware, a recording opens Capture or the Recorder, a quota alert opens AI usage and a new track opens Now Playing.
