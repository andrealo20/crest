A font of your choice, a Report a bug button, and fixes for the setups that 0.3.3 got wrong: the notch on the bottom or a side edge, light themes, longer translations, other keyboards and Windows editions.

- Fonts: Settings → Themes → Font picks the font of the whole app from sixteen, each shown as itself in the menu. Inter stays the default; the others come with Windows, and on a Mac each one uses a Mac font of the same kind.
- Report a bug: below Plugins in Settings. It opens the bug report form on GitHub with your Crest version filled in.
- Bottom and side edges: menus in Settings open upright and in the right place ([#2](https://github.com/andrealo20/crest/issues/2)). On a side edge the open panel slides along the edge again, the privacy dots show in the dimmed column, and in resize mode the grips sit where they should and the Done button is in reach. The panel comes out of its own edge.
- Links: calendar Join buttons (Zoom, Teams and other links with parameters) opened Documents instead of the browser. They open the browser again, and so do files with a comma in the name.
- Light themes: the terminal keeps a dark ground and the video badges in Capture are readable.
- Languages: longer translations fit in Overview, Capture, the calendar and Hardware. Notice times and dates and 12-hour times follow the language. In Arabic, sliders and level bars fill from the left and the clipboard row scrolls the right way.
- Fonts with uneven digits (Gabriola, Ink Free, MV Boli) use Inter's digits, so timers and the clock do not jitter.
- Windows: Crest starts on Windows N editions without the Media Feature Pack, and recording says what is missing. Shortcuts recorded on a German, French or other layout fire on the key you pressed. With a left-handed mouse, leaving focus mode and the drop zone follow the primary button. Pasted images go to your Pictures folder, also when OneDrive moved it.
- Store version: the microphone is recorded.
